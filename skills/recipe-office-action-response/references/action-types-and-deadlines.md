# Office actions: types, posture and deadlines

Read the rule on the office's own page with `flowleap-legal` before you state it in a report. A period that you did not read in this run is a "check" item. Mark each date that you compute "computed by the agent; the attorney confirms".

## US actions

| Type | Code | What the applicant can do |
|---|---|---|
| Non-final rejection | CTNF | Reply with amendments and arguments (37 CFR 1.111); interview |
| Final rejection | CTFR | Amendments only as 37 CFR 1.116 permits (cancel claims, formal matters, better form for appeal, or with a showing of "good and sufficient reasons"); RCE (37 CFR 1.114); notice of appeal; one interview after final is normally permitted (MPEP 713.09) |
| Restriction or election | CTRS | Elect, with or without traverse |
| Ex parte Quayle | CTEQ | Formal corrections only; prosecution on the merits is closed |
| Advisory action | CTAV | Shows whether an after-final reply was entered; the period keeps running |

The After Final Consideration Pilot 2.0 (AFCP 2.0) is closed: the USPTO accepted no request after 14 December 2024. Do not propose it.

Deadlines:

- The shortened statutory period (SSP) is stated in the action, usually 3 months.
- Extensions under 37 CFR 1.136(a) are for up to 5 months with a fee, but never past the 6-month statutory maximum of 35 U.S.C. 133.
- After a final action: when the first reply is filed within 2 months of the mailing date, the extension is counted from the date of the advisory action (MPEP 706.07(f)). Give the 2-month date.
- Write: "SSP <n> months from <date>: <date without extension>; extendable under 1.136(a) to <6-month date>. Computed by the agent; the attorney confirms." Name the date the action states (mailing date or notification date) and use it.

Interviews: MPEP 713. An interview before the first action is normally only for a continuing application (MPEP 713.01, 713.02). The examiner can invite one (Automated Interview Request).

## EP actions

| Type | Where it shows | What the applicant can do |
|---|---|---|
| European search report only (no opinion read) | `citedReferences` of the A1 or A3 | Nothing to answer from the report itself; ask for the search opinion |
| Extended European search report with search opinion | the opinion (a file) | Reply to the opinion under Rule 70a with amendments and arguments; request examination and confirm that the application is maintained (Rule 70(1)/(2)) |
| Euro-PCT: written opinion of the ISA, then Rule 161/162 | WO and the Rule 161 communication | Reply under Rule 161(1) and pay claims fees under Rule 162; the searched claims can be the claims after this reply |
| Communication under Art. 94(3) | a file | Reply with amendments and arguments; informal consultation with the examiner |
| Communication under Rule 71(3) (intention to grant) | a file | Approve the text, or ask for changes |
| Summons to oral proceedings (Rule 115) | a file | Written submissions by the final date set under Rule 116 |

Deadlines and posture:

- Rule 70a: the reply to the search opinion is due within the Rule 70(1) or 70(2) period. Without a reply, the application is deemed to be withdrawn (Rule 70a(3); Guidelines B-XI 8). Give that posture for a search report with an opinion.
- Art. 94(3): the period is set in the communication, normally 4 months. Extension under Rule 132(2) is normally granted only up to 2 more months, 6 months in total (Guidelines E-VIII 1.2 and 1.6.1). A missed period can be cured by further processing (Art. 121): check.
- Check: the notification date rule of Rule 126 changed on 1 November 2023 (the 10-day rule). Do not apply a 10-day addition; ask the attorney to confirm the date of notification.

Informal consultation with the examiner: Guidelines C-VII 2 (check).
